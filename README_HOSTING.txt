v15 CHANGES FROM LEG 44

1. AUTO PACE KEPT ESSENTIALLY AS-IS
Leg 44 stayed synchronized using waypoint confirmations alone, so v15 preserves the
conservative auto-pacing behavior rather than retuning it.

2. CONVERSATIONAL QUIET-STRETCH OUTROS RESTORED
The v14 threshold was so strict that the natural transitions almost never played.
v15 lowers the threshold for selected narration blocks while still requiring the
block to be specifically marked for a bridge.

Examples will vary naturally:
  "Enjoy the view for a little while..."
  "I'll let you take in the scenery for the next few miles..."
  "We have a quieter stretch ahead..."
These are not added after every story.

3. STRONGER VISUAL ANCHORS
When an off-route location may be difficult to identify, narration now tries to give
a second visual reference where the geography supports it:
- Portland: dense city around the Willamette River
- Pearson Field: north bank of the Columbia just east of the I-5 bridge
- Ridgefield: wetlands between the Columbia and developed corridor
- Kalama: port/docks directly on the Columbia waterfront
- Mount St. Helens: the truncated cone itself as the primary reference
- Cowlitz/Toutle: winding or sediment-filled river valleys versus straight I-5
- Chehalis-Centralia: airport between the two town clusters near I-5

When a landmark is too small to identify from altitude, the narration says so rather
than pretending it should be visible. Winlock's giant egg is the deliberate example.

4. EXISTING FEATURES RETAINED
- Tour and Trip remain interchangeable.
- Two-NM waypoint reminder and quiet confirmation window.
- Deliberate Trip/Tour Update with next-story timing.
- First post-takeoff sync preserves the opening story.
- No guessed MSFS/FPL waypoint aliases.
